# Lagos Life

A small life-simulation game set in Lagos, Nigeria. It's a standalone browser
game: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies.
It is not part of the pnpm workspace.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Progress saves to `localStorage`.

`play.html` is the same page without the document wrapper. It's used to publish
the game as a hosted Artifact.

## What's in it

- **Needs:** hunger, energy, hygiene, fun, social and comfort. Together with
  short-lived moodlets they set your mood.
- **Home:** click furniture to use it. Your Sim walks from room to room and
  looks after themselves when left idle (free will).
- **NEPA:** random power cuts switch off the TV, laptop, fan and AC, and spoil
  fridge food. Run the generator on fuel, or buy solar to stop caring.
- **Travel:** go by danfo, BRT or ride-hailing across the Mainland and the
  Island. Rush hour, Third Mainland Bridge and flood days slow you down.
- **Careers:** tech, banking, Nollywood and trading. Each has six levels, and
  promotion depends on performance and a skill.
- **People:** eight Lagosians who keep their own schedules around town. You
  can gist, joke, flirt, date and borrow money.
- **Money:** weekly rent, furniture upgrades, moving up from Yaba to Banana
  Island, and random events like area boys, fuel scarcity and owambe
  invitations.

Controls: click to interact. Space pauses, and 1–3 set the speed.
